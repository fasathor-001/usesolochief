How usage works

Planning is unlimited. AI actions have an allowance.

Building your Weekly Plan, logging Today Focus and running your Friday Review on the web are never limited. Usage allowances apply to AI-assisted actions and WhatsApp — AI Chat, WhatsApp commands and Morning Brief — and reset on the period shown below.

SoloChief limits proactive WhatsApp messages to protect quiet time and prevent notification overload.

Plan questions

Pricing, answered directly

Can I use SoloChief for free?▾

Yes. Free includes the full Web Command Center — Weekly Plan, Today Focus, Follow-up Tracker, Parking Lot, Launch Checklist, Stop List, Switch Challenge and Friday Review — with 20 AI Chat actions a month and one Rescue Me session a week. It does not expire and no card is required.

Is the seven-day trial a full Pro trial?▾

No. The seven-day trial is WhatsApp-specific. It unlocks Pocket Chief commands and Morning Brief at the Pro-tier allowance while you stay on the Free plan. Your web AI Chat allowance and Rescue Me limit are unaffected. See the trial section for details.

Do I need a card for the WhatsApp trial?▾

No. The trial is activated manually from Settings or onboarding and does not require a card.

What happens when the trial ends?▾

WhatsApp access ends automatically after seven days unless you upgrade to Pro or Operator. The trial is one-time only, per account.

What is the difference between Pro and Operator?▾

Operator includes everything in Pro, plus voice-note logging in WhatsApp, a higher AI Chat and WhatsApp command allowance (300 versus 100 a month), and an unlimited Morning Brief allowance instead of Pro's 30 a month.

Does Operator include unlimited everything?▾

Not everything. Morning Brief, commitments and Rescue Me are unlimited on Operator, and AI Chat and WhatsApp commands carry a higher monthly allowance than Pro. Proactive WhatsApp messages are capped the same way on every plan to protect quiet time.

Can I cancel or downgrade?▾

You can manage your subscription from billing settings. Your access follows the active subscription state reported by the billing provider.

Does WhatsApp replace the Web Command Center?▾

No. Pocket Chief in WhatsApp carries the same week into a faster, more immediate place to act on it. The Web Command Center remains the full planning and review system on every plan.
