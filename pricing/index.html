How usage works

Planning is unlimited. AI actions have an allowance.

Building your Weekly Plan, logging Today Focus and running your Friday Review on the web are never limited. Usage allowances apply to AI-assisted actions — WhatsApp messages, Morning Briefs and proactive check-ins — and reset each month. Operator carries the highest allowance for heavier weekly execution.

Plan questions

Pricing, answered directly

Which plan should I choose?▾

Start on Free to organise your week on web. Move to Pro when you want Pocket Chief in WhatsApp. Operator suits heavier weekly execution with the highest usage allowance.

Can I change or cancel my plan?▾

Yes, anytime from Settings. Cancelling keeps access until your current billing period ends. More in the full FAQ.

Is Operator available now?▾

Yes, as an early access plan at $39/month. Some capabilities continue to roll out as the plan matures.
