Is SoloChief free?▾

Yes. The web Command Center is free and fully usable on its own: Weekly Plan, Today Focus, Follow-up Tracker and Friday Review.

How do web and WhatsApp work together?▾

They share one plan and one focus. Pocket Chief, on Pro, carries that same context into WhatsApp.

Does SoloChief replace my existing tools?▾

It replaces the planning and focus layer, not necessarily every tool. Most people run it alongside whatever they already use for deep work.

What happens during the trial?▾

Free is not a time-limited trial. It stays free. Upgrade to Pro or Operator whenever the week needs more.

Is my data secure?▾

Yes. Authentication is handled securely, access is controlled, and your data is never sold or used for advertising.
