Built for people managing too many open loops

SoloChief is a personal Chief of Staff for people who need a clearer operating rhythm. It combines a weekly planner, daily focus tracker, follow-up tracker, morning brief, and AI productivity assistant in one simple system.

Whether you are a founder, operator, freelancer, consultant, builder, or small team lead, SoloChief helps you protect focus, remember commitments, and keep important follow-ups from slipping.

Four steps to a sharper operating rhythm.

1

Plan the week

Set your focus, outcomes, follow-ups, and stop list before the week takes over.

2

Get your morning brief

Chief sends the day's focus and top priorities, right where you already are.

3

Operate during the day

Reply with done, blocked, slipped, switched, or park. Chief logs everything in real time.

4

Review and adjust

Close the week knowing what happened, what slipped, and what needs attention next.
