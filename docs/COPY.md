## No Hype Rule

SoloChief copy makes no unverifiable claims about productivity gains, time saved, or
revenue earned. We do not say "10x your output" or "save 2 hours a day."

The product solves a real problem for a specific person. Describe the problem and the
mechanism. Let the user draw their own conclusion.

---

## No AI Magic Rule

Do not describe the AI in mystical terms. Do not say "our AI understands you" or "powered
by advanced AI" without saying what it actually does.

Acceptable:
> "The AI reads your current commitments, weekly plan, and today's focus before responding."

Not acceptable:
> "Our AI magically understands your workload and gives you personalised insights."

---

## No Overexplaining the Engine Rule

On public-facing pages (landing page, marketing site, social), do not explain the context
package, the four-agent architecture, or the AI_Actions validation pipeline.

The user cares about the outcome: "It helped me figure out what to work on today."
The mechanism is documented internally in `/docs`. It does not belong in the headline.
