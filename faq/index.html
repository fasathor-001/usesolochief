Getting Started

Questions about setup, account creation and whether a download is required.

3 questions
How do I create a SoloChief account?▾

Sign up at solochief.app with your email. No download is required — the Command Center runs in your browser.

Is there an app to download?▾

No app install is required. The Command Center is a web app, and Pocket Chief runs inside WhatsApp, an app you likely already have.

What should I do first after signing up?▾

Build your first Weekly Plan: choose one main focus and commit to three outcomes. That plan is what the rest of the system runs against.

02

Web and WhatsApp

Questions about the Command Center, Pocket Chief, Morning Brief and WhatsApp.

4 questions
What is the Command Center?▾

The web home for planning your week, protecting today's focus and reviewing what happened — available on Free.

What is Pocket Chief?▾

The same operating rhythm inside WhatsApp on Pro — your focus, follow-ups and Morning Brief arrive there instead of waiting for you to open a dashboard.

How do I connect WhatsApp?▾

From Settings, tap Connect WhatsApp and send the pre-filled message to activate it on your number. No separate app or manual setup.

What is the Morning Brief?▾

A daily WhatsApp message on Pro summarising today's focus, priorities and follow-ups due — sent proactively, not something you have to check for.

03

Plans and Usage

Questions about Free, Pro, Operator, usage and upgrades.

3 questions
What's the difference between Free, Pro and Operator?▾

Free covers the full web Command Center. Pro adds Pocket Chief in WhatsApp. Operator adds voice-note logging and the highest usage allowance. See the full comparison.

What counts against my usage allowance?▾

AI-assisted actions such as WhatsApp messages, briefs and check-ins. Core planning and focus tracking on the web are not limited. See How usage works for specifics.

Can I change or cancel my plan?▾

Yes, anytime from Settings. Cancelling keeps access until your current billing period ends.

04

Data and Privacy

Questions about data use, security, account control and deletion.

3 questions
Is my information private?▾

Yes. Authentication is handled securely, access to your data is controlled, and we do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

What does SoloChief remember about me?▾

Your commitments, follow-ups and weekly context, so you don't have to hold it all yourself. The exact retention scope is still being refined — check Settings for current detail.

Can I delete my account and data?▾

Yes, from Settings. Account deletion is available at any time.

05

Product Behaviour

Questions about planning rules, Rescue Me, follow-ups, Friday Review and future features.

4 questions
Why can I only commit to three outcomes a week?▾

SoloChief is built to protect focus, not just track tasks. Three outcomes force a realistic week instead of an overloaded list that nothing actually finishes.

What is Rescue Me?▾

A structured next move for when the day breaks down — it helps identify what can still move instead of forcing the original plan.

What happens to unfinished follow-ups?▾

They stay visible until resolved, moved or deliberately closed, and carry forward into the next Friday Review.

What capabilities are coming later?▾

Communication preferences and Quiet hours are planned but not yet available on any plan.
