Is SoloChief a replacement for Notion?

No. Notion is excellent for notes, documentation, and knowledge management. Keep it if it works for you.

SoloChief solves a different problem: the operating rhythm around your week. Focus, commitments, follow-ups, and a daily brief.

If you use Notion to manage your week and keep dropping the routine, SoloChief is built for that problem.
